About Radon Roster

Radon Roster is an independent directory of radon professionals certified by the Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection (PA DEP). It is built for Pennsylvania homeowners, home buyers and sellers, landlords and real estate agents who need to find a certified radon tester or mitigation contractor, and for radon professionals who want their public listing to be accurate.

What you will find here

  • 414 DEP-certified radon testing, mitigation and laboratory firms, grouped by county and city, each with its DEP certification IDs and a link to the source list.
  • County and city radon statistics computed from PA DEP's public Radon Test Results dataset (radon levels by county).
  • Plain-language guides: what radon is, testing vs mitigation, typical costs and real estate disclosure.

Who operates Radon Roster

Radon Roster (radonroster.com) is operated by Radon Roster, the same operator named in our privacy policy. Contact us through the contact page. Watch our explainer videos on YouTube.

Independence

Radon Roster is not affiliated with PA DEP, EPA or any contractor. A listing is not an endorsement. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment, and any future paid placement will be labeled "Ad". Read how we make money and about our data for the full methodology.

Corrections

Firms can claim, correct or remove a listing. Other questions: see the contact page.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public PA DEP certification lists, retrieved and dated. Not affiliated with DEP; inclusion isn't endorsement.

How they're ordered

By location and name. Any paid placement is labeled "Ad" and never reorders free listings.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the DEP ID, or call the PA Radon Hotline at 800-237-2366.
