# Contact Radon Roster

Radon Roster is a small independent directory of Pennsylvania DEP-certified radon professionals. We read every message, but we are not a radon contractor and cannot test or fix homes ourselves.

## Email

General questions, data corrections and advertising: **hello@radonroster.com**. Please include the listing ID (for example F0207) when writing about a specific firm.

## Claim, correct or remove a listing

Radon professionals can use the [claim, correct or remove form](https://radonroster.com/claim-listing/). Before launch, changes will be verified against the contact details on the PA DEP certification record before anything is edited.

## Radon questions about your home

For official guidance call the PA DEP Radon Hotline at 800-237-2366, or start with our guide [What is radon?](https://radonroster.com/what-is-radon/). To hire help, [browse certified firms by county](https://radonroster.com/pa/counties/).

## Privacy

See our [privacy policy](https://radonroster.com/privacy/) for how messages and form submissions are handled.
