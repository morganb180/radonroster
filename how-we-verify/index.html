How we verify listings

Every firm listed on Radon Roster comes from Pennsylvania DEP's own certification records. Nothing is added from reviews or scraped directories.

On this page

  1. Sources and last retrieval
  2. What we check
  3. What we don't verify
  4. Corrections

Sources and last retrieval

  • PA DEP Radon Services Directory certification lists dated 10/01/2026, retrieved and checked 2026-10-11.
  • Local radon statistics: PA DEP Radon Test Results dataset (PA Open Data Portal), computed 2026-10-11.

What we check

  • Each firm and certified individual appears on the DEP list; DEP certification IDs are shown on every listing with a link to the source.
  • Firms are filed by the county of their DEP business address; firms based outside PA are listed separately.

What we don't verify

  • Workmanship, prices, insurance or availability.
  • Websites, service areas or photos a firm adds later, until the claim process checks them against the contact on the DEP record.

Corrections

Use claim, correct or remove a listing, or see about our data.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public PA DEP certification lists, retrieved and dated. Not affiliated with DEP; inclusion isn't endorsement.

How they're ordered

By location and name. Any paid placement is labeled "Ad" and never reorders free listings.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the DEP ID, or call the PA Radon Hotline at 800-237-2366.
