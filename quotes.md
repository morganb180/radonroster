# Get free quotes from up to 3 certified radon pros near you

One short form. We send it to up to 3 PA DEP-certified firms closest to you that offer the service you need, and show you who they are. They contact you directly, and you decide whether to hire anyone.

## How it works

1. **Tell us what you need.** Pick a service and enter your ZIP code and how firms can reach you.
2. **We send it to up to 3 nearby firms.** DEP-certified firms in your county or the counties next to it, closest first, that offer that service. Firms can't pay to be picked.
3. **They contact you. You compare.** You see their names right away and get the list by email. Compare written quotes; hire anyone or no one.

## What happens to your info

* **Who gets it:** up to 3 PA DEP-certified firms that offer the service you chose, nearest available first, starting with your county and the counties next to it. If you came from a firm's listing, that firm is one of them. Firms can't pay to be picked.
* **What they get:** one email per firm with your name, email and phone (if given), ZIP, county, the service, and any note. When a firm replies, it goes to you.
* **What you get:** their names and phone numbers on screen right away, plus the same list by email from leads@radonroster.com.
* **What we keep:** a copy of each request email in the Radon Roster inbox. Our server log has no name, email or phone: just a reference number, date, county, first 3 ZIP digits, service and firm IDs, kept about 13 months.
* **What we don't do:** sell your details, call or text you, or send you marketing unless you tick the optional box.
* **Delete it anytime:** email hello@radonroster.com and we'll delete our copy. Firms keep what they received under their own policies.

Full details: [privacy policy](https://radonroster.com/privacy/).
