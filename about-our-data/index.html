About our data

  • Contractor listings: parsed from PA DEP's certified testing, mitigation and laboratory PDF lists dated 10/01/2026, linked from the DEP Radon Services Directory and retrieved 2026-10-11. We combined DEP's alphabetical and county-sorted lists. DEP's PDFs cut off long names and emails, so some entries look shortened. ZIP codes aren't in DEP's lists. Phone numbers are reformatted only.
  • Radon levels: computed from PA DEP's Radon Test Results dataset (PA Open Data Portal, public domain). ZIP search maps each ZIP to the county where it most often appears in that dataset.
  • No endorsement: A listing is not a recommendation, and we're not affiliated with PA DEP. Always confirm current certification with DEP (800-237-2366).
  • Firm grouping: DEP lists firms and their certified individuals as separate entries. We group them by shared business email domain or by the same street address and city. Names cut off by DEP's 35-character field were completed only when the firm's own website or another DEP list confirmed the full name; the rest are flagged. Websites were added only after loading the firm's own site. Full change log: data/cleanup-log.csv.
  • City data: city figures use the municipality/mailing name in DEP's test dataset.
  • Corrections: claim, correct or remove a listing.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

How they're ordered

By location and name. Any paid placement is labeled "Ad" and never reorders free listings.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the DEP ID, or call the PA Radon Hotline at 800-237-2366.
