# Radon testing & mitigation in Allentown, PA

Allentown sits in Lehigh County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 29,724 basement radon tests carry a Allentown address. Their median was **3.6 pCi/L**, and **47%** came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Lehigh County it was 47%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Below are the PA DEP-certified firms with a business address in Allentown, then other firms in Lehigh County, then mitigation firms in the counties next door. Certified pros often travel, so ask each one whether they cover your street.

City figures come from the municipality / mailing-address name in [PA DEP's Radon Test Results](https://data.pa.gov/Energy-and-the-Environment/Radon-Test-Results-September-1986-Current-Annual-C/vkjb-sx3k) (computed 2026-10-11). They count tests, not homes.
