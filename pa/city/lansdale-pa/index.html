Answers

How common is high radon in Lansdale, PA?

25% of 19,617 basement radon tests with a Lansdale address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 2.2 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Lansdale?

Radon Roster lists 3 PA DEP-certified firms based in Lansdale, 26 more elsewhere in Montgomery County and 18 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.

How much does radon mitigation cost near Lansdale?

We don't have Lansdale-specific price data. PA DEP says most radon mitigation systems typically cost $800-$1,200. Get written quotes from DEP-certified mitigators.
