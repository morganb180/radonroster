# Radon testing & mitigation in Stroudsburg, PA

Stroudsburg sits in Monroe County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 7,508 basement radon tests carry a Stroudsburg address. Their median was **3.8 pCi/L**, and **49%** came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Monroe County it was 46%, and statewide 37%. That is above the county share, so testing matters here.

**How common is high radon in Stroudsburg, PA?**

49% of 7,508 basement radon tests with a Stroudsburg address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 3.8 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

**Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Stroudsburg?**

**What radon level should be fixed?**

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.
