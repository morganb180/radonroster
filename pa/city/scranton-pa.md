# Radon testing & mitigation in Scranton, PA

Scranton sits in Lackawanna County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 9,215 basement radon tests carry a Scranton address. Their median was **1.8 pCi/L**, and **20%** came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Lackawanna County it was 28%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Below are the PA DEP-certified firms with a business address in Scranton, then other firms in Lackawanna County, then mitigation firms in the counties next door. Certified pros often travel, so ask each one whether they cover your street.

City figures come from the municipality / mailing-address name in [PA DEP's Radon Test Results](https://data.pa.gov/Energy-and-the-Environment/Radon-Test-Results-September-1986-Current-Annual-C/vkjb-sx3k) (computed 2026-10-11). They count tests, not homes.
