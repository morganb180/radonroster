Radon testing & mitigation in Greensburg, PA

Quick answer: For homeowners, buyers and sellers in Greensburg, PA: 39% of 15,223 basement radon tests with a Greensburg address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level, about the same as Westmoreland County (40%) and above the statewide 37%. Test first; if you're at 4 pCi/L or higher, hire a PA DEP-certified mitigator. Radon Roster lists 3 certified firms based in Greensburg, and you can request free quotes through Radon Roster.

Greensburg sits in Westmoreland County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 15,223 basement radon tests carry a Greensburg address. Their median was 3.1 pCi/L, and 39% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Westmoreland County it was 40%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in Greensburg: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Greensburg, PA?

39% of 15,223 basement radon tests with a Greensburg address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 3.1 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Greensburg?

Radon Roster lists 3 PA DEP-certified firms based in Greensburg, 14 more elsewhere in Westmoreland County and 6 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.

How much does radon mitigation cost near Greensburg?

We don't have Greensburg-specific price data. PA DEP says most radon mitigation systems typically cost $800-$1,200. Get written quotes from DEP-certified mitigators.
