Radon testing & mitigation in Altoona, PA

Quick answer: For homeowners, buyers and sellers in Altoona, PA: 39% of 7,523 basement radon tests with a Altoona address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level, lower than Blair County (43%) and above the statewide 37%. Test first; if you're at 4 pCi/L or higher, hire a PA DEP-certified mitigator. Radon Roster lists 1 certified firm based in Altoona, and you can request free quotes through Radon Roster.

Altoona sits in Blair County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 7,523 basement radon tests carry a Altoona address. Their median was 2.8 pCi/L, and 39% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Blair County it was 43%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in Altoona: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Altoona, PA?

39% of 7,523 basement radon tests with a Altoona address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 2.8 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Altoona?

Radon Roster lists 1 PA DEP-certified firm based in Altoona, 2 more elsewhere in Blair County and 4 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.

How much does radon mitigation cost near Altoona?

We don't have Altoona-specific price data. PA DEP says most radon mitigation systems typically cost $800-$1,200. Get written quotes from DEP-certified mitigators.
