Radon testing & mitigation in Stroudsburg, PA

Stroudsburg sits in Monroe County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 7,508 basement radon tests carry a Stroudsburg address. Their median was 3.8 pCi/L, and 49% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Monroe County it was 46%, and statewide 37%. That is above the county share, so testing matters here.

Below are the PA DEP-certified firms with a business address in Stroudsburg, then other firms in Monroe County, then mitigation firms in the counties next door. Certified pros often travel, so ask each one whether they cover your street.

City figures come from the municipality / mailing-address name in PA DEP's Radon Test Results (computed 2026-10-11). They count tests, not homes.
