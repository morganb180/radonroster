Radon testing & mitigation in Easton, PA

Easton sits in Northampton County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 22,144 basement radon tests carry a Easton address. Their median was 3.6 pCi/L, and 47% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Northampton County it was 48%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in Easton: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Easton, PA?

47% of 22,144 basement radon tests with a Easton address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 3.6 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Easton?

Radon Roster lists 3 PA DEP-certified firms based in Easton, 4 more elsewhere in Northampton County and 11 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.

How much does radon mitigation cost near Easton?

We don't have Easton-specific price data. PA DEP says most radon mitigation systems typically cost $800-$1,200. Get written quotes from DEP-certified mitigators.
