Radon testing & mitigation in Carlisle, PA

Quick answer: For homeowners, buyers and sellers in Carlisle, PA: 55% of 15,932 basement radon tests with a Carlisle address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level, about the same as Cumberland County (55%) and above the statewide 37%. The median Carlisle result, 4.8 pCi/L, is itself over the line. Test first; if you're at 4 pCi/L or higher, hire a PA DEP-certified mitigator. Radon Roster lists 1 certified firm based in Carlisle, and you can request free quotes through Radon Roster.

Carlisle sits in Cumberland County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 15,932 basement radon tests carry a Carlisle address. Their median was 4.8 pCi/L, and 55% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Cumberland County it was 55%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in Carlisle: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Carlisle, PA?

55% of 15,932 basement radon tests with a Carlisle address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 4.8 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Carlisle?

Radon Roster lists 1 PA DEP-certified firm based in Carlisle, 5 more elsewhere in Cumberland County and 8 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.
