Radon testing & mitigation in State College, PA

Quick answer: For homeowners, buyers and sellers in State College, PA: 55% of 23,460 basement radon tests with a State College address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level, about the same as Centre County (56%) and above the statewide 37%. The median State College result, 4.8 pCi/L, is itself over the line. Test first; if you're at 4 pCi/L or higher, hire a PA DEP-certified mitigator. Radon Roster lists 1 certified firm based in State College, and you can request free quotes through Radon Roster.

State College sits in Centre County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 23,460 basement radon tests carry a State College address. Radon in State College: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in State College, PA?

55% of 23,460 basement radon tests with a State College address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 4.8 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near State College?

Radon Roster lists 1 PA DEP-certified firm based in State College, 3 more elsewhere in Centre County and 2 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.
