Radon testing & mitigation in York, PA

Quick answer: For homeowners, buyers and sellers in York, PA: 58% of 28,440 basement radon tests with a York address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level and above the statewide 37%. The median York result, 5.0 pCi/L, is itself over the line. Test first; if you're at 4 pCi/L or higher, hire a PA DEP-certified mitigator. Radon Roster lists 5 certified firms based in York, and you can request free quotes through Radon Roster.

York sits in York County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 28,440 basement radon tests carry a York address. Their median was 5.0 pCi/L, and 58% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of York County it was 56%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in York: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in York, PA?

58% of 28,440 basement radon tests with a York address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 5.0 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near York?

Radon Roster lists 5 PA DEP-certified firms based in York, 3 more elsewhere in York County and 13 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.

How much does radon mitigation cost near York?

We don't have York-specific price data. PA DEP says most radon mitigation systems typically cost $800-$1,200. Get written quotes from DEP-certified mitigators.
