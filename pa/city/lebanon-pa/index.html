Radon testing & mitigation in Lebanon, PA

Lebanon sits in Lebanon County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 10,794 basement radon tests carry a Lebanon address. Their median was 6.6 pCi/L, and 60% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Lebanon County it was 60%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in Lebanon: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Lebanon, PA?

60% of 10,794 basement radon tests with a Lebanon address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 6.6 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Lebanon?

Radon Roster lists 2 PA DEP-certified firms based in Lebanon, 2 more elsewhere in Lebanon County and 12 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.

How much does radon mitigation cost near Lebanon?

We don't have Lebanon-specific price data. PA DEP says most radon mitigation systems typically cost $800-$1,200. Get written quotes from DEP-certified mitigators.
