# Radon testing & mitigation in Pittsburgh, PA

Pittsburgh sits in Allegheny County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 157,738 basement radon tests carry a Pittsburgh address. Their median was **2.6 pCi/L**, and **31%** came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Allegheny County it was 34%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Below are the PA DEP-certified firms with a business address in Pittsburgh, then other firms in Allegheny County, then mitigation firms in the counties next door. Certified pros often travel, so ask each one whether they cover your street.

City figures come from the municipality / mailing-address name in [PA DEP's Radon Test Results](https://data.pa.gov/Energy-and-the-Environment/Radon-Test-Results-September-1986-Current-Annual-C/vkjb-sx3k) (computed 2026-10-11). They count tests, not homes.
