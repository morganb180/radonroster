Radon testing & mitigation in Phoenixville, PA

Quick answer: For homeowners, buyers and sellers in Phoenixville, PA: 27% of 16,364 basement radon tests with a Phoenixville address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level, lower than Chester County (31%) and below the statewide 37%. Lower odds aren't a pass: neighboring houses can test very differently. Test first; if you're at 4 pCi/L or higher, hire a PA DEP-certified mitigator. Radon Roster lists 3 certified firms based in Phoenixville, and you can request free quotes through Radon Roster.

Phoenixville sits in Chester County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 16,364 basement radon tests carry a Phoenixville address. Their median was 2.2 pCi/L, and 27% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Chester County it was 31%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in Phoenixville: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Phoenixville, PA?

27% of 16,364 basement radon tests with a Phoenixville address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 2.2 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Phoenixville?

Radon Roster lists 3 PA DEP-certified firms based in Phoenixville, 15 more elsewhere in Chester County and 16 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.
