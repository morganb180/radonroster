Radon testing & mitigation in Wilkes-Barre, PA

Quick answer: For homeowners, buyers and sellers in Wilkes-Barre, PA: 18% of 6,150 basement radon tests with a Wilkes-Barre address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level, lower than Luzerne County (31%) and below the statewide 37%. Lower odds aren't a pass: neighboring houses can test very differently. Test first; if you're at 4 pCi/L or higher, hire a PA DEP-certified mitigator. Radon Roster lists 1 certified firm based in Wilkes-Barre, and you can request free quotes through Radon Roster.

Wilkes-Barre sits in Luzerne County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 6,150 basement radon tests carry a Wilkes-Barre address. Their median was 1.7 pCi/L, and 18% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Luzerne County it was 31%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in Wilkes-Barre: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Wilkes-Barre, PA?

18% of 6,150 basement radon tests with a Wilkes-Barre address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 1.7 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Wilkes-Barre?

Radon Roster lists 1 PA DEP-certified firm based in Wilkes-Barre, 12 more elsewhere in Luzerne County and 9 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.
