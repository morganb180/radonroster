Radon testing & mitigation in Philadelphia, PA

Philadelphia sits in Philadelphia County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 144,527 basement radon tests carry a Philadelphia address. Their median was 1.6 pCi/L, and 12% came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Philadelphia County it was 12%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Radon in Philadelphia: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Philadelphia, PA?

12% of 144,527 basement radon tests with a Philadelphia address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 1.6 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Philadelphia?

Radon Roster lists 10 PA DEP-certified firms based in Philadelphia and 13 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.

How much does radon mitigation cost near Philadelphia?

We don't have Philadelphia-specific price data. PA DEP says most radon mitigation systems typically cost $800-$1,200. Get written quotes from DEP-certified mitigators.
