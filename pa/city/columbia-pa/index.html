Radon testing & mitigation in Columbia, PA

Quick answer: For homeowners, buyers and sellers in Columbia, PA: 55% of 2,129 basement radon tests with a Columbia address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level, about the same as Lancaster County (55%) and above the statewide 37%. The median Columbia result, 4.8 pCi/L, is itself over the line. Test first; if you're at 4 pCi/L or higher, hire a PA DEP-certified mitigator. No certified firms list a Columbia address, but Radon Roster lists firms elsewhere in Lancaster County and next door, and you can request free quotes through Radon Roster.

Radon in Columbia: frequently asked questions

How common is high radon in Columbia, PA?

55% of 2,129 basement radon tests with a Columbia address in PA DEP's records were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level (median 4.8 pCi/L). Statewide the share is 37%. Only a test tells you your home's level.

Which DEP-certified radon firms are in or near Columbia?

Radon Roster lists 0 PA DEP-certified firms based in Columbia, 9 elsewhere in Lancaster County and 16 mitigation firms in neighboring counties. Check any firm's cert ID in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory or call 800-237-2366.

What radon level should be fixed?

EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher and considering a fix between 2 and 4 pCi/L.
