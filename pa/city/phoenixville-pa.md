# Radon testing & mitigation in Phoenixville, PA

Phoenixville sits in Chester County. In the PA DEP test dataset, 16,364 basement radon tests carry a Phoenixville address. Their median was **2.2 pCi/L**, and **27%** came in at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level. Across all of Chester County it was 31%, and statewide 37%. Even where the average is lower, neighboring houses can test very differently, so every home should be tested.

Below are the PA DEP-certified firms with a business address in Phoenixville, then other firms in Chester County, then mitigation firms in the counties next door. Certified pros often travel, so ask each one whether they cover your street.

City figures come from the municipality / mailing-address name in [PA DEP's Radon Test Results](https://data.pa.gov/Energy-and-the-Environment/Radon-Test-Results-September-1986-Current-Annual-C/vkjb-sx3k) (computed 2026-10-11). They count tests, not homes.
