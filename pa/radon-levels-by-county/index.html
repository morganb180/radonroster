Computed 2026-10-11 from PA DEP's Radon Test Results, September 1986 - Current (PA Open Data Portal, public domain). Statewide, 37% of 2,644,033 basement tests were at or above 4 pCi/L. These are tests reported to DEP, not a random survey: repeat and post-mitigation tests are included. Basement readings usually run higher than living-floor readings.
