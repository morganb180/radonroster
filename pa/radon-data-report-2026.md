# Which Pennsylvania counties test highest for radon?

Data report, October 2026. Fulton, Juniata and Perry counties had the highest share of basement radon tests at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level in PA DEP's public test records. Statewide, 37% of 2,644,033 basement tests crossed that line.

## Press summary

Quotable findings (Radon Roster analysis of PA DEP radon test data, retrieved 2026-10-11):

1. 37% of Pennsylvania basement radon tests reported to DEP (985,388 of 2,644,033) were at or above EPA's 4 pCi/L action level.
2. Fulton County had the highest share: 70% of 707 basement tests at or above 4 pCi/L, nearly 6 times the rate in Philadelphia (12%), the lowest.
3. In 22 of Pennsylvania's 67 counties, at least half of all basement radon tests were at or above 4 pCi/L.
4. Even in Philadelphia, the lowest-share county, 18,896 basement tests were at or above 4 pCi/L.
5. 3 of the 10 counties with the highest shares (Fulton, Juniata and Jefferson) have no DEP-certified radon firm with a business address in the county.

## Methodology

- Data: PA DEP, [Radon Test Results, September 1986 - Current](https://data.pa.gov/Energy-and-the-Environment/Radon-Test-Results-September-1986-Current-Annual-C/vkjb-sx3k), county aggregates retrieved 2026-10-11.
- Measure: basement results at or above 4.0 pCi/L divided by all basement results, per county. First floor computed the same way.
- Statewide: 985,388 of 2,644,033 basement results (37%), test-weighted. First floor: 78,826 of 396,546 (20%).
- Ranking by unrounded share; shown rounded. Medians are DEP basement results as aggregated at retrieval.
- Certified firms: firms with a business address in the county on PA DEP certification lists dated 10/01/2026.
- Limits: tests reported to DEP, not a random sample of homes; repeat, real estate and post-mitigation tests included; small counties swing more.
