How do I hire a radon pro in Monroe County, PA?

With 46% of 39,824 basement tests at or above 4 pCi/L (above the 37% statewide share), Monroe County has 3 DEP-certified mitigation firms based locally.

  • Verify: check the firm in the PA DEP Radon Services Directory, ask for the DEP ID (25 Pa. Code Chapter 240), or call 800-237-2366.
  • Typical cost: $800-$1,200 for a mitigation system, national range from the HomeAdvisor cost guide.
  • Selling or buying: PA's seller disclosure form asks about known radon and past tests (49 Pa. Code § 35.335a).
