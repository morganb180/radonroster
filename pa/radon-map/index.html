How to read this map

  • Darker counties had a larger share of basement tests at or above 4 pCi/L, the level at which EPA recommends fixing a home.
  • The map shows tests reported to DEP, not a random survey of homes. Counties with few tests (for example, Forest, Cameron and Sullivan have under 1,000 each) can swing more.
  • A county average says nothing certain about one house. Only a test tells you your home's level. Find a DEP-certified tester in your county.
