# Privacy policy

Updated Oct 30, 2026. This policy covers Radon Roster (radonroster.com, "we"), operated by Radon Roster, 26141 Red Corral Rd, Laguna Hills, CA 92653.

**What changed on Oct 18, 2026:** Radon Roster now keeps a copy of each quote request (the details you submit, the firms it went to and the date) to run and improve the service. We don't sell it. Quote forms also have a separate, optional box (unchecked by default) for radon tips and offers from Radon Roster; we only send those to people who check it. See "Radon tips and offers" below.

## Quote requests

* **What you enter:** your name, email and/or phone, ZIP code or county, the service you need, and optionally your radon test result and a note.
* **Who gets it:** when you check the consent box and submit, we connect you with **up to 3 PA DEP-certified radon firms** that offer that service. For testing and mitigation, those are the nearest available firms, starting with your county and the counties next to it. Mail-in kit labs can be anywhere on the PA DEP lab list. If you came from a firm's listing, that firm is included. Firms can't pay to be picked. You see their names on screen after you send the form. They contact you directly. We also keep a copy in our inbox, described below.
* **What we keep:** we keep a copy of each quote request: the details you submit (your name, email and/or phone, ZIP code or county, the service you need, and any test result or note), the firms it went to and the date. We use it to run and improve the service, and we don't sell it. We only use it to send you radon tips and offers if you opt in (below). Our server also keeps routing records: a request ID, the date and time, county, first three digits of your ZIP, the service requested and which firms were contacted. These records are kept for up to about 13 months to run and improve the service.
* **How a request is delivered:** we connect you with the matched firms. SendGrid (Twilio), a service provider, delivers that request and keeps delivery logs under its own retention policy. Firms that receive your request keep it under their own policies.
* **Spam protection:** forms use Cloudflare Turnstile and per-connection rate limits. To rate-limit, we keep a one-way hash of your IP address for about an hour.

## Radon tips and offers (optional)

* **Only with your opt-in:** the quote form has a second, optional box, unchecked by default: "Also send me radon tips and offers from Radon Roster." Your quote request goes through whether or not you check it.
* **What we store if you opt in:** your name, email, phone (if you gave one), ZIP code, the service you asked about and the date you opted in, plus which firm you picked (if any), the page you used and the version of the opt-in wording you agreed to. We use it to send you radon tips and offers from Radon Roster. We don't store your IP address with it.
* **Unsubscribe or delete:** you can unsubscribe at any time, or ask us to delete your details, by emailing hello@radonroster.com.
* **Not sold:** we don't sell this list.

## Clicks on firm contact links

Firm phone numbers, email addresses, and websites are not published on Radon Roster. A quote request is how you reach a firm from this site.

## Listing claims and corrections

We use the contact and business details you send to verify and process listing changes.

## Usage data

Our host keeps standard server logs. We don't use advertising cookies.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect aggregate usage statistics (pages viewed, the place searches you enter, how the search and quote or listing forms are used, and which listing links are clicked) so we can improve the site. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies and receives your IP address and browser details; we never send it your name, email, phone, address or anything you type into a form field. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalization, and we don't use it for advertising. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, Google Analytics doesn't load at all. See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## What we don't do

* We don't sell your personal information.
* We don't share quote requests with anyone other than the matched firms and our service providers (Cloudflare for hosting and form processing, SendGrid for email delivery, Google for the email inbox where we keep our copy of each request).
* We don't text or call you, and firms don't get permission to text you through this form.

## Contractors

Business listings come from public PA DEP certification records (see [About our data](https://radonroster.com/about-our-data/)). Firms receive the requests we connect them with, and every request has a one-click link to stop receiving them. Firms can [claim, correct or remove](https://radonroster.com/claim-listing/) their listing.

## Your choices

Questions or deletion requests: email hello@radonroster.com or use the [contact page](https://radonroster.com/contact/). Email us to have our copy of your quote request deleted, or, if you opted in to radon tips and offers, to unsubscribe or have your stored details deleted. We can't retrieve or delete what the firms received; contact the firms listed in your confirmation email.

## Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the date above. Material changes to how quote requests are shared will be highlighted at the top of this page before they take effect.
