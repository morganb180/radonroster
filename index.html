Our advice

Test before you call a mitigator.

Radon varies house to house, even on the same street. A test costs far less than a system, and it tells you whether you need one at all.

Testing vs. mitigation, explained →

Get two written quotes.

Ask each certified mitigator for their DEP certification ID, and retest after the system is installed. A national cost guide puts typical systems at $786–$1,274.

Typical costs and estimate tool →

Use a kit analyzed by a certified lab.

Pennsylvania requires labs that analyze radon for the public to be DEP-certified. Follow the kit's placement and timing instructions, then mail it promptly.
