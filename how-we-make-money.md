# How we make money

* **Free listings for every certified firm.** Every firm on PA DEP's certified lists is listed free. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment.
* **Labeled ads (possible in future).** If we ever sell a featured slot, it will be labeled **"Ad"**, kept separate from the free listings, and open only to firms with current DEP certification.
* **Quote-request fees (possible in future).** When you ask for quotes, participating certified contractors may pay us a fee for the request. Using the service is free for you, with no obligation to hire.
* **What payment doesn't buy:** a "certified" badge (that comes only from DEP records), changes to radon data, reviews or editorial content, or removal of competitors.
