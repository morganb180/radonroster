For radon contractors

Every PA DEP-certified radon firm already has a free listing here, built from DEP's public certification lists. Free listings are sorted by Google rating, weighted by number of reviews, never by payment. “Top rated” marks firms with 4.5+ stars from 20 or more Google reviews.

What you can do now

  • Claim your listing to add a website or service area, correct details, or ask us to remove it.
  • Get a free certification badge for your website. It shows your DEP certification type and links to your listing so homeowners can verify it.
  • Check that your DEP certification is current: we only list firms on DEP's lists.

Quote requests

Homeowners can ask for quotes from up to 3 nearby certified firms. If we offer paid options for contractors in the future, they will be labeled, and they will never change the free list or the "certified" badge. See how we make money.
